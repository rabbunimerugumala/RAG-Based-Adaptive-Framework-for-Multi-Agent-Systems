College Central Library - Rules for Students (sample document)
This is a fictional sample document created for this project.

1. Opening hours
The central library is open from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM on weekdays and from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM on Saturdays.
The library is closed on Sundays and on public holidays.

2. Borrowing
Undergraduate students may borrow up to 4 books at a time for a period of 14 days.
Postgraduate students may borrow up to 6 books for 21 days.
Reference books, journals and project reports cannot be taken out of the library.

3. Renewal and fines
A book may be renewed once if no other student has reserved it.
A fine of 2 rupees per day is charged for each overdue book.
Lost books must be replaced, or the student must pay twice the current price of the book.

4. Digital resources
Students can access e-journals from the campus network using their college email ID.
Off-campus access requires the library VPN, which can be requested at the help desk.

5. Conduct
Mobile phones must be kept on silent mode. Food is not allowed inside the reading hall.
Group discussion is permitted only in the discussion rooms on the first floor.
